# 04 – Operating Model

## **4.1 Purpose of the Operating Model**

The operating model defines **how the national incubator network functions day to day**.  
It translates the system architecture into **processes, workflows, roles, and operational mechanisms** that ensure consistency, scalability, and high‑quality execution.

The goal is to create an incubator network that is:
- evidence-based (MCF 2.1)  
- capability-aligned (IMM-P®)  
- future-oriented (Vigía Futura)  
- operationally disciplined  
- globally integrated  

---

## **4.2 Core Operating Principles**

The operating model is built on six principles:

1. **Evidence Before Action**  
   Every stage requires documented evidence using MCF‑aligned templates.

2. **Maturity-Aligned Execution**  
   Programs are adapted to the ecosystem’s IMM-P® maturity level.

3. **Consistency Across Incubators**  
   Unified processes, shared templates, and common reporting standards.

4. **Public–Private Symmetry**  
   Balanced operational responsibilities and decision‑making.

5. **Data-Driven Governance**  
   KPIs, dashboards, and transparent reporting.

6. **Foresight Integration**  
   Programs updated annually with Vigía Futura signals.

---

## **4.3 The Operating Cycle**

The national incubator network operates through a continuous, modular cycle:

1. **Outreach & Pipeline Development**  
2. **Application & Selection**  
3. **Incubation (MCF Discovery & Validation)**  
4. **Acceleration (Traction & Scaling)**  
5. **Investment & Capital Deployment**  
6. **Monitoring & Reporting**  
7. **Graduation & Globalization**  
8. **Feedback Loop & Foresight Update**

Each module is described below.

---

## **4.4 Module 1 — Outreach & Pipeline Development**

Activities:
- Awareness campaigns  
- University and research institution partnerships  
- Corporate partner scouting  
- Diaspora founder outreach  
- Global startup attraction programs  

Outputs:
- Monthly pipeline report  
- Founder database  
- Sector‑aligned recruitment (Vigía Futura)

---

## **4.5 Module 2 — Application & Selection**

### **Selection Criteria (aligned with MCF 2.1 Value Architecture)**

1. Clear problem definition  
2. Evidence of customer need  
3. Early validation signals  
4. Team capability assessment  
5. Market opportunity size  
6. Alignment with sector priorities  
7. Technical feasibility  
8. Scalability potential  
9. Ethical, regulatory, and safety considerations  

### **Selection Process**

- Initial screening by Technical Operating Unit  
- Evaluation committee scoring  
- Interviews + MCF evidence review  
- Final approval by governance board

Outputs:
- Selection report  
- Evidence-based scoring sheets  
- Cohort composition

---

## **4.6 Module 3 — Incubation Program (MCF 2.1)**

The incubation phase focuses on **discovery and validation**.

### **Core Components**

1. **MCF 1.x – Problem Architecture**  
2. **MCF 1.4 – Customer Analysis**  
3. **MCF 2.x – Solution Design & Value Architecture**  
4. **MCF 3.x – Evidence Requirements**  

### **Activities**

- Weekly cohort sessions  
- 1:1 coaching  
- Mentor interactions  
- Customer interviews  
- Validation sprints  
- Product discovery cycles  

### **Outputs**

- Evidence dossier  
- Validated problem–solution fit  
- Initial product roadmap  
- Go/No‑Go recommendations

---

## **4.7 Module 4 — Acceleration Program (Traction & Scaling)**

Focuses on growth, revenue, and investment readiness.

### **Components**

- Traction milestones  
- Sales pipeline development  
- Pricing models  
- Digital marketing infrastructure  
- Unit economics  
- Investment readiness workshops  
- Global expansion readiness  

### **Outputs**

- Growth metrics  
- Investor data room  
- Scaling plan  
- Co‑investment eligibility

---

## **4.8 Module 5 — Investment & Capital Deployment**

The network coordinates with the National Innovation Fund and private investors.

### **Funding Stages**

1. **Seed Grants**  
2. **Matching Investments**  
3. **Convertible Notes / SAFE**  
4. **Co‑Investment Mechanisms**

### **Processes**

- Due diligence  
- Investment committee approval  
- Tranche‑based disbursement  
- Performance monitoring  

Outputs:
- Funding agreement  
- Investment decision memo  
- Tranche progression reports

---

## **4.9 Module 6 — Monitoring, Reporting & Governance**

### **Reporting Requirements**

Startups submit quarterly:

- KPIs  
- Financials  
- Evidence updates  
- Customer traction metrics  
- Progress against milestones  

### **Incubator Reporting**

Each incubator reports to the Technical Operating Unit:

- Program compliance  
- Cohort performance  
- Mentor effectiveness  
- Funding utilization  

---

## **4.10 Module 7 — Graduation & Globalization**

Activities include:

- Global partner matching  
- Trade mission integration  
- Cross‑border accelerator referrals  
- International soft‑landing programs  
- VC introductions  
- Diaspora market entry pathways  

Outputs:
- Globalization strategy for each startup  
- Partnerships activated  
- Export readiness score  

---

## **4.11 Module 8 — Feedback Loop & Foresight Integration**

Each annual cycle concludes with:

- System‑wide review  
- KPI and outcome evaluation  
- IMM-P® maturity rescore  
- Update of sector priorities using Vigía Futura  
- Policy update recommendations  
- Program redesign proposals  

This ensures the incubator network remains **adaptive**, **evidence-based**, and **aligned with future opportunities**.
