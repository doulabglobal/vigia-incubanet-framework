# 07 – KPIs & Scorecard

## **7.1 Purpose of the KPI Framework**

The KPIs and scorecard system ensures that the national public–private incubator network operates with **transparency, discipline, and measurable outcomes**.

It integrates:

- **MCF 2.1 Evidence Indicators**  
- **IMM-P® Maturity Metrics**  
- **Vigía Futura Foresight Indicators**  

The objective is to track ecosystem performance, startup progression, capital efficiency, and long-term sector impact.

---

## **7.2 Principles of the KPI System**

1. **Evidence-Based Measurement**  
   KPIs are tied to validated outputs, not assumptions.

2. **Comparability**  
   Metrics are standardized across all incubators.

3. **Leading + Lagging Indicators**  
   Combine early signals with long-term outcomes.

4. **Sector Sensitivity**  
   KPIs can be segmented by priority sectors.

5. **Transparency & Accountability**  
   Metrics reported quarterly and annually.

6. **Alignment with National Goals**  
   KPIs mapped to development and competitiveness objectives.

---

## **7.3 KPI Categories**

KPIs are divided into seven major categories:

1. **Startup Pipeline & Density**
2. **Incubation & Acceleration Performance**
3. **Capital & Investment Metrics**
4. **Talent Development Indicators**
5. **Commercialization & Market Access**
6. **Governance & Institutional Strength**
7. **Foresight & Future Competitiveness (Vigía Futura)**

Each category is detailed below.

---

## **7.4 Category 1 — Startup Pipeline & Density**

### **Core KPIs**
- Number of applications per cycle  
- Acceptance rate  
- Startup density per region  
- International startup participation  
- Sector distribution alignment (Vigía Futura)  

### **Why it matters**
Indicates ecosystem attractiveness, awareness, and inclusiveness.

---

## **7.5 Category 2 — Incubation & Acceleration Performance**

### **Core KPIs**
- Evidence completion rate (MCF 2.1)  
- Customer interviews completed  
- Problem–solution fit validation  
- Prototype delivery rate  
- Cohort completion rate  
- Time-to-validation  

### **Acceleration Indicators**
- Traction milestones achieved  
- Revenue growth  
- User growth  
- Partnership activation  

---

## **7.6 Category 3 — Capital & Investment Metrics**

### **Core KPIs**
- Total capital deployed  
- Public-to-private investment ratio  
- Follow-on investment secured  
- Matching investment multiplier  
- Time to decision (funding approval cycle)  
- Tranche completion rate  

### **Capital Efficiency**
- Cost per validated startup  
- Cost per job created  
- Return on public capital  

---

## **7.7 Category 4 — Talent Development Indicators**

### **Core KPIs**
- Professionals trained  
- Skill progression (pre/post assessments)  
- University-to-startup pipeline indicators  
- Diaspora talent integration  
- Female and underrepresented founder participation  

### **Talent Retention**
- Retention rate 12–36 months post-program  

---

## **7.8 Category 5 — Commercialization & Market Access**

### **Core KPIs**
- Export readiness score  
- International partnership activations  
- Global market entry events  
- Corporate innovation engagements  
- Tech transfer agreements signed  

### **Commercial Outcomes**
- Revenue from global customers  
- Number of international pilots  

---

## **7.9 Category 6 — Governance & Institutional Strength**

### **Core KPIs**
- Reporting compliance (incubators + startups)  
- Governance meeting attendance  
- Time-to-decision for committees  
- Audit pass rate  
- Stakeholder coordination effectiveness  
- Policy update cycle adherence  

These indicators help track institutional maturity, supporting IMM-P® scoring.

---

## **7.10 Category 7 — Foresight & Future Competitiveness (Vigía Futura)**

### **Core KPIs**
- Alignment with future sector opportunities  
- Weak-signal detection frequency  
- Adoption of updated sector maps  
- Participation in foresight exercises  
- Sector competitiveness improvements  

### **Impact Over Time**
- Priority sectors gaining investment  
- Sector-specific job creation  
- Increase in exportable IP and deep-tech ventures  

---

## **7.11 National Scorecard Structure**

The national scorecard consolidates all KPIs into five high-level indices:

1. **Startup Performance Index**  
2. **Ecosystem Maturity Index (IMM-P®)**  
3. **Innovation Capital Index**  
4. **Future Competitiveness Index (Vigía Futura)**  
5. **Governance Integrity Index**

Each index is a weighted combination of KPIs.

---

## **7.12 Reporting Mechanisms**

### **Quarterly Reporting**
- Dashboard updates  
- Evidence logs (MCF 2.1)  
- Funding utilization reports  
- KPI performance summaries  

### **Annual Reporting**
- Ecosystem maturity re-score  
- National competitiveness review  
- Investment performance report  
- Foresight alignment update  

Reports are reviewed by the National Steering Council.
