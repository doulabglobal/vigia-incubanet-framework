# 00c  -  Glossary of Terms  
## Vigía Incubation Framework (VIF)
**Version 1.0  -  Vigía Incubation Framework**

This glossary establishes a unified language for all institutions, incubators, investors, universities, and government agencies implementing the Vigía Incubation Framework (VIF). Definitions are concise, operational, and aligned with internationally recognized terminology.

---

# **A. Core Methodologies**

### **MCF 2.1 (MicroCanvas Framework)**
An evidence-based venture development methodology that standardizes problem analysis, customer insights, solution validation, and business model evaluation. Used across all incubator nodes.

### **IMM-P® (Innovation Maturity Model Program)**
A capability and institutional maturity model assessing readiness, governance, and execution capacity across public and private organizations.

### **Vigía Futura**
A national foresight engine that identifies emerging sectors, signals, and long-term opportunities, guiding strategic prioritization within VIF.

---

# **B. Governance Concepts**

### **National Steering Council (NSC)**
High-level governance body responsible for national strategy, cross-sector coordination, annual priorities, and oversight of the national incubator network.

### **Technical Operating Unit (TOU)**
Central execution team responsible for operations, data, MEL, accreditation, reporting, and ecosystem coordination.

### **Investment Committee (IC)**
Independent, mixed-sector body evaluating venture readiness, approving funding tranches, and ensuring risk-aligned investments.

---

# **C. Incubation & Program Concepts**

### **Incubator Node**
Any participating organization (public, private, academic, or international) accredited to run programs under VIF standards.

### **Evidence Package**
A structured set of validated insights produced using MCF 2.1, used to evaluate startup decisions, risk, and investment readiness.

### **Maturity Assessment**
An evaluation of an institution’s innovation capacity and governance using IMM-P® scoring.

### **Sector Prioritization**
The process by which Vigía Futura determines future-aligned priority sectors each year.

---

# **D. Funding & Investment Terms**

### **National Innovation Fund (NIF)**
The central investment mechanism funding incubators, startups, and innovation programs under VIF.

### **Co-investment Mechanism**
Funding structure where public and private investors jointly invest in vetted startups following standardized due diligence.

### **Tranche-Based Disbursement**
A milestone-based investment approach where funds are released only when specific evidence requirements and KPIs are met.

### **SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)**
An early-stage investment instrument used for simplicity, transparency, and alignment with global innovation ecosystems.

---

# **E. MEL (Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning)**

### **KPI System**
A standardized set of metrics used to assess incubator performance, startup progress, governance maturity, and national outcomes.

### **National Dashboard**
A unified platform displaying MEL, performance data, and KPIs across incubator nodes and national programs.

### **Annual Performance Cycle**
Yearly continuous-improvement system that integrates MEL outcomes, foresight insights, and policy adjustments.

---

# **F. Data, IP & Compliance**

### **Data Governance Framework**
Rules regulating the collection, sharing, usage, and protection of data across VIF.

### **IP Agreement**
Standardized contracts ensuring clarity on ownership, co-creation, and commercialization rights for startups and institutions.

### **PPP MOU (Public–Private Partnership Memorandum of Understanding)**
Agreement defining roles, responsibilities, data terms, and collaboration rules between public and private incubator nodes.

---

# **G. Foresight & Strategy**

### **Weak Signals**
Early indicators of potential future changes detected through Vigía Futura’s scanning system.

### **Horizon Scanning**
Systematic exploration of emerging trends and disruptions across sectors.

### **Scenario Exploration**
Structured development of plausible future states to anticipate risks and opportunities.

---

# **H. Operational Standards**

### **Accreditation**
The process through which incubators are approved to join the national network, based on capability, maturity, and alignment with VIF.

### **Program Standardization**
A requirement that all incubator nodes use the same tools, templates, evidence structures, and reporting protocols.

### **Governance Score**
A composite indicator of institutional readiness, decision-making quality, and compliance with national governance rules.

---

# **I. National Impact Concepts**

### **Ecosystem Diagnostic**
A structured analysis of the country’s innovation ecosystem, including actors, gaps, opportunities, and readiness for network deployment.

### **Policy Feedback Loop**
Mechanism by which MEL data and foresight insights update policies annually, creating an adaptive national innovation system.
