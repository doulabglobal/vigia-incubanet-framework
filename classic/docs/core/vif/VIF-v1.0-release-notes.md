## **Release Notes  -  Version 1.0 (Stable)**  
### **Date: 2025‑11‑17**

---

# 🎉 **VIF v1.0  -  Official Stable Release**

VIF v1.0 marks the first **complete, standardized, and deployment‑ready** version of the **Vigía Incubation Framework**, a comprehensive methodology for designing, governing, operating, funding, and scaling national public–private incubation networks.

This release includes:

- a fully unified governance model,  
- standardized evidence and maturity systems,  
- end‑to‑end operational templates,  
- legal and compliance frameworks,  
- data governance and transparency structures,  
- national MEL (Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning) architecture,  
- and complete annex documentation ready for government deployment.

This version is now considered **stable**, **implementable**, and **ready for adoption by national authorities**.

---

# 🚀 **1. What’s Included in v1.0**

Version 1.0 comprises all primary documentation and annexes required to deploy and operate a national incubation network.

## **Core Sections (00–11)**  
These include:

- Executive Summary  
- Introduction  
- Ecosystem Diagnostic  
- System Architecture  
- Operating Model  
- Funding Model  
- Benchmarking  
- KPIs & Scorecard  
- Roadmap  
- Governance & Legal Framework  
- Templates  
- References

All sections now follow a consistent structure, terminology, and licensing standard.

# 📐 **3. Governance Integration Completed**

VIF v1.0 includes full and consistent definitions of:

- **NSC**  -  Strategic authority  
- **TOU**  -  Operational authority and national data processor  
- **IC**  -  Independent investment body  

Governance boundaries are now:

- clear,  
- strict,  
- auditable,  
- and resistant to institutional misuse.

---

# 📊 **4. Evidence, Maturity & MEL Aligned**

This release fully integrates:

- **MCF 2.1** as the national evidence standard  
- **IMM-P®** as the national maturity progression model  
- **Vigía Futura** as the national foresight and learning system  

MEL has been aligned with OECD, UNDP, and World Bank standards.
